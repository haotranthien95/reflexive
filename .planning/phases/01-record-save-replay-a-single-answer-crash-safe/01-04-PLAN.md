<objective>
Close verification Gap 1 (the practice loop's unrecoverable dead ends) and Gap 3 (unguarded failure paths that freeze the loop) so that no reachable state leaves the user on a screen with a question and no way forward — and back both with the first real tests the two riskiest units have ever had.

**User story (MVP mode):** **As a** learner drilling spoken English, **I want to** always have a visible way to move the practice loop forward — even when the recorder, the disk or the player misbehaves — **so that** a stumble in the machinery never costs me the answer I just spoke or forces me to kill and relaunch the app.

Purpose: `flutter test` reports 32/32 green today, yet three of the five phases the loop can reach render no control at all, and the class that owns the 60s deadline and the first-stop-wins guard has zero tests. This plan makes "the loop always reaches a recoverable state" a mechanically enforced property rather than a hope.

Output: a `PracticePhase.arming` state that stops the UI lying about the microphone; a total phase→control mapping proven exhaustively over `PracticePhase.values`; a `RecordingService` whose deadline survives a losing stop; a bounded replay wait; and three new test files.
</objective>
