<objective>
Expand the proven tracer slice into the full multi-question drill: after every committed
answer the loop optionally replays, counts 3-2-1 into the next question, and terminates
automatically once `questionCount` answers have been committed. Adds the second countdown
surface (the 96px determinate ring for `t`) and finishes the completion state's copy and
actions.

Purpose: LOOP-07 and LOOP-08 are what make a *session* a session rather than a single
answer. This plan is where the identity model promoted in 02-01 gets exercised at N.

Output: a session of any configured length that runs unattended from START SESSION to
"Nice work!", with N durable per-question writes and no counter drift.
</objective>
